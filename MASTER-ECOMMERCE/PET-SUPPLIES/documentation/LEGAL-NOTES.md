# Legal notes - NOT LEGAL ADVICE: verify everything with a lawyer

Policy pages in this repo are templates. The points below are issues to raise with counsel; they are not statements of law.

- **Prescription / regulated veterinary products:** the store flags categories `rx` and products `rx: true`; they are shown for information, cannot be added to cart, get no price/Offer markup, and COD refuses carts containing them. Rules on veterinary drugs (e.g. Drugs and Cosmetics Act schedules, veterinary prescription requirements, online pharmacy rules) must be checked before listing any medicine, tick/flea treatment or therapeutic diet. Only OTC/supplement-type examples are seeded.
- **Pet food labelling / FSSAI:** check whether FSSAI registration/licensing applies to your pet-food activities and what labelling (manufacturer, batch, dates, ingredients, veg/non-veg marks, net quantity) must appear on listings. Pet food regulation is evolving; the demo shows batch/expiry/manufacturer/origin fields but values are placeholders.
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules / e-commerce disclosures:** MRP inclusive of taxes, net quantity, manufacturer/packer/importer details and country of origin are expected on pre-packed goods and in e-commerce listings; selling price must not exceed MRP (the importer enforces this). Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules require seller details, grievance officer, return/refund/shipping information and no fake reviews - the store shows no invented reviews or ratings.
- **DPDP Act 2023:** consent at registration (privacy checkbox) and separate optional marketing consent are recorded with timestamps; pet details are voluntary. You will need notice wording, withdrawal/erasure flows, retention schedule, processor agreements (gateway, couriers), breach procedure and, if applicable, grievance officer. There is no erasure UI yet.
- **Subscriptions / recurring payments:** RBI e-mandate rules require explicit customer consent and mandate registration (additional factor authentication, pre-debit notification, limits). Recurring collection is **disabled**; schedules only send reminders. Use gateway subscription products (Razorpay Subscriptions / UPI AutoPay) and have the flow reviewed first.
- **COD, GST, invoices:** GST invoicing, HSN codes, place-of-supply and e-invoicing thresholds are not implemented. COD cash handling/reconciliation is operational, not coded.
- **Gift cards / loyalty points:** prepaid-instrument rules may apply to stored value; points here are a discount ledger only and gift cards are not implemented.
- **Health claims:** copy avoids medical claims; keep it that way (supplements and coat products are labelled as non-medicated). Pet matching uses only type/age group/size and states it is not veterinary advice.
- **Brand/trademark:** demo brands are "Demo Brand A-F" with no real marks. "Paw & Hearth" is unvetted.
